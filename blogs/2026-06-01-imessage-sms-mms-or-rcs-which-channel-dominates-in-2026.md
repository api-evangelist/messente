This article compares four mobile messaging channels, iMessage, SMS, MMS, and RCS, examining their features, security, reach, and usage patterns. It concludes that SMS remains the most widely accessible channel due to universal phone compatibility, while RCS is experiencing rapid growth as the future of cross-platform rich messaging.
