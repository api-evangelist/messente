SlickText is an SMS marketing platform that helps businesses grow subscriber lists, send bulk messages, and run automated campaigns. Its opt-in forms and keyword campaigns are especially popular with small and medium-sized businesses seeking to engage customers via text. Many customers seek alternatives to SlickText due to the following issues: Costs increase significantly as subscriber numbers grow, making pricing less predictable and more expensive for larger lists.
