Everyday messaging has evolved from traditional SMS to apps like WhatsApp and, more recently, RCS—which isn’t an app at all, but rather a global, developing messaging infrastructure. Both RCS and WhatsApp look and act similarly on the surface. They both let you send rich messages with images, videos, and files, and each works across all modern smartphones.
