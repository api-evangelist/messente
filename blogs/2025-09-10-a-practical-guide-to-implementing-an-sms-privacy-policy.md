In March 2025, Designer Shoe Warehouse (DSW) agreed to pay $4.42 million to settle a class action suit related to marketing texts sent to customers who had opted out of marketing communications. This was not an isolated incident in recent years. As SMS marketing grows and becomes more widespread around the world, the actual risk is not just fines, but trust, reputation, and years of remediation.
