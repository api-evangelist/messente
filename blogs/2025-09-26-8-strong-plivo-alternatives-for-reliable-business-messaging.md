Plivo is a cloud communication platform that lets companies integrate voice calling and SMS messaging via simple APIs. It’s popular for its global reach, with direct connections to 1,600+ carrier networks across nearly 195 countries, and a flexible pay-as-you-go pricing model that makes it a cost-effective alternative to several other larger providers. Many businesses have chosen Plivo for its competitive rates.
