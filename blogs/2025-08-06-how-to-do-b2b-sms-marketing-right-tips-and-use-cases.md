Everyone talks about SMS in a B2C context, but some of the most critical texts go not to consumers, but to business users. When an OTP fails to deliver, or a partner misses an urgent update, revenue slips through the cracks. While SMS may not be the flashiest tool in your marketing stack, it gets the job done when emails are ignored and timing is critical.
